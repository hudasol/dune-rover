## Recommended order of submission

1. **First choice: IEEE/RSJ IROS 2027 (deadline 1 Mar 2027).** It is the best topic fit and prestige level for a field robotics benchmark in six page IEEE format, and the deadline leaves four months. The cost is travel to Florence, so apply for any student travel support once the 2027 site is up.
2. **Second choice: IEEE ICAR 2027 (deadline 21 Mar 2027).** Use it instead of IROS if the draft is not ready by 1 Mar. It is IEEE RAS co sponsored, takes six page IEEE papers and suits mobile robotics. Do not submit the same work to both at once.
3. **Fallback: ISTVS 2027 (call not out; the 2026 cycle closed abstracts on 3 Apr and papers on 15 May).** It is the most specialised audience for dune slip faces and off road mobility, and its spring cycle fits after an IROS decision. For a quick, low cost regional IEEE Xplore option instead, ETA 2027 in Bahrain (deadline 20 Jan 2027) is the only Gulf call found. Confirm its dates and remote presentation with the organisers first.
