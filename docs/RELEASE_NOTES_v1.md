# Release notes, versions 1.0, 1.1 and 1.2

## Version 1.2 (2026-10-28): baselines, benchmark and conference paper

- **Benchmark (SIMULATED BY ME on PUBLISHED data; stages 25 and 26 registered before any metric was computed).** The planner's slip face detector against six baselines (slope, ruggedness, plane roughness, cubic interpolation, gradient boosted trees and a small convolutional network, the last two trained leave one site out on lidar labels) on six lidar dune fields, with 10 m and 30 m block means and the real Copernicus GLO-30 product as inputs and block bootstrap intervals; the shipped planner with each hazard layer swapped in, on 600 origin and destination pairs scored on face patches in the lidar. Stage 25: 7 predictions hit, 2 missed; stage 26: 5 hit, 3 missed. At 30 m the cubic interpolation ranks faces best or tied best and the detector beats only plain slope; no computable 30 m hazard layer plans many face free routes, although the better ones halve the length of route on faces; a face map from fine data makes even a 30 m grid safe (post hoc).
- **Conference paper.** `paper/paper.pdf` (six pages, IEEE conference format) generated from `paper/src/` and the result files by `scripts/build_paper.py`, with 43 references checked against Crossref or official pages, a fresh review with 20 findings and the response to each (`docs/REVIEW_2026-10-28.md`), and post hoc analyses decided after that review (`scripts/benchmark_posthoc.py`). Venues with deadlines read on official pages: `docs/VENUES.md`.
- **Product.** Exports for robot software (Nav2 occupancy map, float GeoTIFF layers, KML, QGroundControl missions); a ROS 2 node that publishes the map and serves `GetPlan`; negative coordinates and `--version` on the command line; one version source; both climbing limits printed beside the Dumont field curve.
- **Engineering.** CI on Python 3.10, 3.12 and 3.13 on Ubuntu and 3.13 on Windows, an install job with pyproj, checks of the paper source in the gate and CI.
- **Documents.** A five line README opener with a figure, `docs/PARTNER_INDEX.md`, `docs/REPRODUCE.md`, report section 16; working logs moved to `docs/process/`.


## Version 1.1 (2026-10-23): the Dune Rover Planner

- **The product (SIMULATED BY ME).** `src/dunerover/planner.py`, `report.py` and `cli.py`: an installable command line tool and Python API (`pip install .`, command `dunerover`) that reads a dune elevation model as a GeoTIFF (geographic or projected) and a vehicle profile, and returns a slip face risk map, the three class map, routes under the naive, conservative and risk weighted policies, a GeoTIFF class map, GeoJSON routes, a summary file and a self contained HTML report.
- **Resolution gate.** Every assessment is labelled RESOLVED, DETECTOR or COARSE; coarser than 10 m, TRAVERSABLE is reported as not verified, with the miss rates measured on the lidar dune fields. The numbers come from `src/dunerover/data/evidence_card.json`, generated from the result files and checked in the gate and CI.
- **Vehicle profiles.** Built in assumed and published posterior profiles; `dunerover vehicle calibrate` fits a measured profile to the rover's own slope runs from the field kit, with bootstrap ranges.
- **Demonstration.** `demo/web/dune_rover_planner.html` (generated by `scripts/build_web_demo.py`) shows four lidar dune fields as 10 m and 30 m maps with routes and the true slip faces; report section 15 and `docs/PLANNER.md` describe the tool and what it cannot do. A demonstration, not a registered test.
- **Fix.** A generated sentence about the Bekker Wong family printed "n/a" for the logistic comparison; corrected, with a test that no generated sentence holds a missing value.
- **Proposals.** The robotics and EDGE proposals and the brief now present the planner as the deliverable the field project calibrates and checks.

## Version 1.0 (2026-10-21)

Author: Huda Mueen. Repository `hudasol/dune-rover`, all rights reserved (see `LICENSE` and `LICENSE-DOCS.md`). This release marks the end of the desk study. It is not field evidence and not engineering approval for any vehicle. Nothing has been sent to anyone.

### What version 1.0 contains

- **Evidence (PUBLISHED).** A verified evidence table (`research/evidence.csv`) with a measured extraction error rate, every row re checked against its full text, and the scaling law literature added on 2026-10-21.
- **Published curves (PUBLISHED).** Slip against slope curves digitised reproducibly in two rounds (`research/phase3/`, `research/phase4/`), with a repeatability check of a random third of the second round.
- **Model validation (SIMULATED BY ME against PUBLISHED curves).** Leave one study out tests of four slip model families and two families built on the granular scaling laws, on two rounds of curves and with post hoc regrouping by campaign and laboratory; none passes the bar fixed in advance (stages 15, 21 and 22 of `docs/PREDICTIONS.md`).
- **Independent simulation (SIMULATED BY ME).** Project Chrono Soil Contact Model sweeps and a calibration on the Dumont Dunes curve; the calibrated run is numerically unstable on steeper slopes, so no extrapolation test was obtained. A discrete element notebook for a free GPU is prepared, not run.
- **Terrain (SIMULATED BY ME on PUBLISHED data).** The slip face detector and three class map scored against one metre lidar of six North American dune fields; route planning policies scored on the same truth; ICESat-2 laser profiles compared with the 30 m map over three Arabian windows, with a method that failed its own validation and post hoc noise screens.
- **Closed loop (SIMULATED BY ME).** Online slip estimation and replanning against a modelled rover, in Python and as ROS 2 nodes; after two reviews, replanning shows no benefit distinguishable from a stop and retry control.
- **Field kit (PLANNED).** Data sheets, a one command analysis tested on a synthetic fixture only, a preregistration draft, drone survey and sand test protocols, and a second rater pack.
- **Documents.** `manuscript/MANUSCRIPT.md`, `docs/REPORT.md`, `docs/EXECUTIVE_BRIEF.md`, `docs/PROPOSAL_ROBOTICS.md`, `docs/PROPOSAL_EDGE.md`, `docs/COMPLETENESS.md`, all generated from result files; every number of the manuscript, brief and proposals is listed in `docs/NUMBER_LEDGER.csv`.
- **Reviews.** Fresh reviewer reports and the response to each finding: `docs/REVIEW_2026-10-15.md`, `docs/REVIEW_2026-10-18.md`, `docs/REVIEW_2026-10-20.md`, `docs/REVIEW_2026-10-21.md`.

### Known limitations at version 1.0

- No field data of the author's own; no measured slip curve for any vehicle on Arabian dune sand.
- Arabian terrain is seen only through satellite laser profiles with a method that failed its own validation.
- The Bekker Wong family was rerun on the expanded curve set and did worse than in the first round; the calibrated Chrono model is not numerically stable above 12.5 degrees, so no extrapolation test of it exists.
- Human spot checks and a second human rater are prepared but not done.
- Source PDFs and raw downloads are not redistributed; the digitisation and the terrain analyses can be rerun only after fetching them (`docs/DATA_AND_LICENSES.md`, `scripts/fetch_lidar.sh`).

## How to check a release

Run `bash scripts/check_all.sh` from the repository root; it runs the tests, rebuilds every generated document from the committed results and checks that nothing changed, checks the number ledger, the repository hygiene and the secrets scan. `bash scripts/clean_clone_check.sh` repeats the rebuild from a fresh clone; its record is in `results/reproducibility/clean_clone_checks.txt`.
