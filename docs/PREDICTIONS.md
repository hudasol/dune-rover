## Stage 2: sub grid slip face detector, three class map, Monte Carlo (committed 2026-10-12 BEFORE any of this code was run)

Pre-specified design (fixed now so the outcome cannot tune it):

- Synthetic ensemble: straight-crested asymmetric dune profiles, stoss slope 6 to 20 deg (published ranges for barchan and transverse dunes, morphology rows PM01 to PM06), active slip face 29.5 to 32.5 deg (PM18, lidar), face height fraction of dune height 0.54 to 1.0 (PM08, small dunes 7 to 10 m; extended to 0.2 to 1.0 for tall dunes as an ASSUMPTION), apron below the face at the stoss slope (ASSUMPTION), height 5 to 120 m log uniform, random phase. The share of crests with an active face is not published (not found), so baseline pi = 1 (worst case) with pi = 0.3 and 0.6 as sensitivities.
- Truth in the synthetic ensemble: "an active face (slope of 28 deg or more) lies inside the cell footprint".
- Features per cell: Horn slope, curvature along the steepest direction, slope break (largest slope difference to the 8 neighbours), local relief in a 7 by 7 window. Model: logistic regression, trained per posting (30, 60, 90 m, box mean sampling; point sampling as a sensitivity).
- Map classes per cell. Monte Carlo: 256 Latin hypercube draws of (phi, f_r, i0, i_max) over the existing ranges, seeds 20261014 (area A), 20261015 (area B), 20261016 (area C). For each draw, a cell is "passable" if at least 4 of its 8 headings are passable. BLOCKED if passable in at most 5 % of draws; TRAVERSABLE if passable in at least 95 % of draws AND the face probability is below 0.20; UNKNOWN otherwise (two causes recorded: assumption sensitive, and sub grid face risk). The 0.20 threshold is a free choice and is swept in the coverage versus certainty curve.

## Stage 10: amended entrapment rule for the 2026-10-15 ladder (written 2026-10-18, BEFORE the amended rule was coded or run)

Amended rule (applies to the 2026-10-15 design):
- Slip test, unchanged: at least 80 % of held out runs within the tolerance (the larger of 3 times the pilot sigma and 0.05); uninformative flag unchanged.

- **E. Closed loop with local sensing.** 30 pairs per site (the first 30 of E1 and of E2). Outcomes: success (goal reached and the driven polyline, scored on the lidar, touches no face patch), stuck (no route left), driven length over straight distance, number of replans.

## Stage 31: POST HOC analyses after the fourth blind review (written 2026-10-29, BEFORE any of them was run)

**POST HOC, SIMULATED BY ME on PUBLISHED data.** Decided after every result of stages 25 to 30 was known (Y30-9 and Y30-10 still running), in response to a fourth blind review of the paper (weak reject): the closed loop tests one eye height, no range noise and one detection model, uses a fixed weight w = 5 while the open loop chooses the weight on the other five sites, mission success ignores what the vehicle can climb, route intervals resample six sites and overlapping pairs, two free 30 m products cited in the paper (AW3D30, FABDEM) were never scored, and the learned baselines are small. While this entry was written, only the availability of the two products was checked (an HTTP status request to the Planetary Computer and to the FABDEM mirror; no elevation was read). These analyses are post hoc; the predictions below are written before the runs to keep the habit and carry no confirmatory weight.

Design (`scripts/fetch_benchmark_stage31.py`, `scripts/benchmark_stage31.py`, outputs in `results/benchmark/stage31/`):
- **(a) Sensor variants of the line of sight closed loop.** As stage 30 part a (I30, E1 endpoints, 20 pairs per site, R = 20 m, w = 5, posterior vehicle, target 0.2 m above the cell centre, crop margin 600 m), priors slip model only, BICUBIC-cal, GBM and FACEMAP, with (i) eye heights 0.5 m and 1.5 m; (ii) range noise: the face patches the rover senses are recomputed with the registered rule from the lidar with independent Gaussian noise of standard deviation 0.05 m or 0.15 m added to every 1 m post (seed 20261029 plus the site index), so noise creates false faces and hides real ones; visibility uses the noise free surface and success is scored against the true face patches; (iii) per observation detection: each face cell is detected with probability 0.8 independently every time it is in view (an undetected cell can be detected at a later step), priors slip model only, GBM and FACEMAP.
- **(b) Weight chosen on the other five sites in the closed loop.** Line of sight as in stage 30 part a (eye 1 m), E1 endpoints, priors BICUBIC-cal, GBM and FACEMAP on I30 and BICUBIC-cal and FACEMAP on I10, w in {1, 5, 20, 100} (w = 5 reused from stage 30 part a); for each held out site the weight with the highest closed loop success on the other five sites is used; difference against the slip model prior with the two stage interval.
- **(c) Vehicle success for every cell of the planning table.** The stage 28 held out operating points (posterior vehicle, both policies) of slip model only, SLOPE-cal, BICUBIC-cal, DET, GBM, CNN, DEM+S2 and FACEMAP on I10 (E1, E2), I30 (E1, E2) and G30 (E1) are replanned and every route is scored by the vehicle rule of stage 30 part b (5 m steps on the 5 m lidar block mean; infeasible when the nominal posterior slip is infinite or above the maximum slip, or the step descends 28 degrees or more); vehicle success = mission success and feasible. The operating points stay those chosen for mission success. Each route is also scored against the labels P300 (patches of at least 300 posts), L60, H and M of stages 29 and 30, and mission and vehicle success are reported by tertile of the straight line distance of the pair (pooled tertile edges) and by site. Route overlap: for the slip model only routes on I30 (E1), the share of route cells (30 m) that at least one other route of the same site also uses, and the mean pairwise Jaccard index of the route cell sets.
- **(d) Site level inference.** For every layer against the slip model in (c) (mission and vehicle success) and in stage 30 part a: the six per site mean differences, their mean, a t interval with five degrees of freedom, and the exact two sided sign flip p (64 sign patterns; smallest possible p = 1/32). No correction is applied; the paper reports these intervals instead of the two stage bootstrap marks. Detection: block bootstrap intervals (300 m blocks, 1000 draws, as Table S4) of the per site AUC of SR and DEM+S2 on I30, and of their paired difference against BICUBIC.
- **(e) Two more free 30 m products.** **A30**: JAXA ALOS World 3D 30 m (AW3D30, a digital surface model from optical stereo), from the Microsoft Planetary Computer collection `alos-dem`; **F30**: FABDEM v1.2 (GLO-30 with forest and building heights removed, University of Bristol), read from the public mirror `links-ads/fabdem` on Hugging Face (pixel values stated to be unchanged). Each is cropped on its own grid, then interpolated bilinearly at the centres of the 30 m cells of each window, as GLO-30 in stage 25. Detection: SLOPE, BICUBIC, DET and GBM (leave one site out) against L-ANY, macro AUC. Planning on E1: slip model only and BICUBIC-cal (leave one site out Platt), weight policy with w in {1, ..., 1000} chosen on the other five sites; mission and vehicle success.
- **(f) A stronger learned baseline.** **UNET** on I30: a U-Net with two downsampling levels (32, 64 and 128 channels, two 3 x 3 convolutions with batch normalisation and ReLU per level), inputs elevation minus its 7 x 7 mean divided by the posting, slope, and the BICUBIC maximum slope; binary cross entropy against L-ANY; Adam at 0.001; batches of 16 crops of 32 x 32 cells with flips and quarter turns; leave one site out, with one of the five training sites (the next in the site order) held for validation: the validation AUC is computed every 500 steps up to 12000 steps, training stops after four evaluations without improvement, and the weights with the best validation AUC are kept; then retrained on all five training sites for the chosen number of steps; seed 0. Macro AUC on I30; planning on I30 (E1 and E2), weight policy with w in {1, ..., 1000} chosen on the other five sites, mission and vehicle success.
- **(g) Summaries without new runs.** How often the chosen operating point lies at the edge of its grid (stage 28 and stage 30 part d); the sensitivity vehicle (stage 28 assumed profile) as a table; the dates of the imagery and of the lidar per site, from the data manifest.
