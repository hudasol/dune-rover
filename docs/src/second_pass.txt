A separate subagent that had not written the report checked the prose of this report (everything before Appendix A) against `research/evidence.csv`, `research/raw/*` and `results/*`, after the draft was complete. It examined 225 claim groups (every number in the simulation tables was compared individually and the statistics were recomputed): 188 correct, 26 minor (right number but a wrong label, row identifier or imprecise wording), 5 wrong and 6 unverifiable (WRONG rate 2.2 %; wrong plus minor 13.8 %). The five wrong items were a kc statement that belonged to another source (TM-029), percentage units applied to drawbar errors given in newtons (TM-001 to TM-004), a data-coverage cell that belonged to another product, a UAE vehicle date, and a tilt-bed row called a Mars measurement (FLD-006). **All 31 wrong and minor items were corrected in this text**; the six unverifiable statements were softened or removed. The full finding table is `research/factcheck_report_pass.md`. This checker is also a language-model agent working from the repository files, so it shares any error that is present in those files; it cannot catch a mistake that traces back to a source misread twice.
