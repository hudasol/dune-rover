# Review of the Dune Rover Planner (2026-10-23)

A fresh reviewer, acting as a sceptical field robotics and terramechanics reviewer who had not seen the work being produced, checked the planner code, its tests, the web demonstration, the documents that present it and the pitch deck (commits 58318d6 to 6a2394a). The reviewer ran the tests, ran the command line on lidar and synthetic inputs, rebuilt the demonstration for Kelso and White Sands (identical to the committed metrics) and recomputed every number in the deck, the user guide, report section 15, the robotics proposal and the README from the result files; all of those numbers matched. The findings and what was done:

| # | Severity | Finding | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | major | With a fine input at its own posting, the 10 square metre face rule could never be met (a 1 m cell holds 1 square metre), so RESOLVED mode found no faces. | Fixed: for cells smaller than 10 square metres the rule is applied over a moving 10 square metre footprint and the cell takes the maximum; test at native posting added. |
| 2 | major | Voids were filled with the mean elevation, so routes could cross them and their edges became false cliffs. | Fixed: a NO DATA class (more than half of a cell's posts void) that no policy routes through; voids filled with the nearest valid elevation for the slope computation; test added. |
| 3 | major | Descents were never checked, although lee faces are the main hazard. | Fixed: a warning for descents of 25 degrees or more, and the conservative and risk weighted policies never descend 28 degrees or more between cells (a product addition, not part of stage 17); test added. |
| 4 | moderate | The deck said every test was registered and named three reviews. | Fixed: "main tests registered before they ran; post hoc analyses labelled"; reviews described without a count. |
| 5 | moderate | The field calibrated profile was labelled MEASURED although i_max is assumed, f_r and i0 were sampled independently, and no held out check was made. | Fixed: label "MEASURED f_r and i0 ...; i_max ASSUMED"; bootstrap pairs drawn jointly; error on runs above 10 degrees reported when there are at least three; test added. |
| 6 | moderate | The user guide left out that no policy was reliably safer than naive at 30 m in the registered test (stage 17). | Fixed in the user guide, report 15.1, the HTML report, the readiness row of the proposal and the deck. |
| 7 | moderate | A GeoTIFF without GeoKeys could be read as degrees; units other than metres were ignored. | Fixed: no GeoKeys means a local frame in metres with a warning; projected units other than metres are refused; a TIFF without geotags gives a clear error; tests added. |
| 8 | minor | Raw errors on a missing file, a TIFF without geotags or start equal to goal; posting rounding was silent. | Fixed with clear messages and a note when the posting is rounded. |
| 9 | minor | The class GeoTIFF referred to parameter tags it did not write. | Fixed: GeoDoubleParams and GeoAsciiParams are carried over; test added. |
| 10 | minor | A fine input analysed at a coarse posting gave no warning on grades. | Fixed: a warning that grades on a coarse grid understate the grades the rover meets. |
| 11 | minor | Deck wording (the 10 m figure, the Chrono instability range, a ROS claim, "the only curve", missing labels). | Fixed. |
| 12 | minor | The ask slide left out the outside reviewer. | Fixed. |
| 13 | minor | A viewer meta tag in the demonstration named an assistant; a lower case sentence start in the EDGE proposal. | Fixed. |
| 14 | minor | The Great Sand Dunes tile was not found by the reviewer, so that site's demonstration numbers were not recomputed. | The tile is listed in `scripts/fetch_lidar.sh` with its checksum; the full demonstration was rebuilt after the fixes with all four fields, and the metrics did not change. |
