## Rules carried into every step

Only full text sources; never invented values; free tools and data only; raw downloads and PDFs stay out of git; generated files come from scripts; no change to existing simulation behaviour or results except new variants beside them; local checks (tests, hygiene, secrets, writing, stale generated files) before every push; nothing is sent to anyone.
