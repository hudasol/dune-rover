Where can a small wheeled robot drive on dune sand, and how sure can a map be? The planner classifies every cell as traversable, blocked or unknown, plans routes three ways, and refuses to call coarse ground safe. Below, the same lidar dunes are assessed as a 10 m and a 30 m map, with the slip faces the one metre lidar shows drawn on top.
