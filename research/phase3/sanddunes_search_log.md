## Inclusion and exclusion

Included: full text sources with numbers for dune sand grain size, sorting, mineralogy, density limits, friction angle, penetration resistance, moisture; dune height, spacing, interdune width, migration rate; station drift potential and resultant drift direction; DEM vertical error over dune terrain. Arabian Peninsula sources first; one non Arabian source (Taklimakan DEM accuracy) is kept only because no Arabian DEM over dune study could be retrieved, and it is flagged as a proxy.

Excluded after reading or screening:
- Boudia and Berga 2021, Civil Engineering Journal 7(8), Algerian Great Western Erg dune sand direct shear: outside the Arabian Peninsula scope.
- Geotechnical data compilation for evaporitic rocks in Abu Dhabi (Data in Brief 2024, PMC10973577): rock, not dune sand.
- Al-Alusi, Sharp, Sainak 2012, Ground improvement in sabkha, Abu Dhabi (ISSMGE): sabkha, no dune sand data.
- van Herpen and Karreman, Dubai calcareous sand shell correction (ISSMGE) and Lambarki et al., CPT response of carbonate sand in Abu Dhabi (ISSMGE): marine dredged reclamation sand, not dune sand.
- Kajiyama, Nakata, Nakase 2023, angle of repose and grain size distribution (ISSMGE IS-Porto): Japanese test sands, not Arabian.
- Narteau group papers P33 (Tengger landscape experiment) and P41 (phase diagrams of dune shape): not Arabian data.
- Li et al. 2025, Remote Sensing 17, 3779 (Kubuqi dune height change by InSAR) and Jacobsen 2026 ISPRS (free DEMs and ICESat-2): no dune specific accuracy relevant to Arabia, or no dunes.
- Uuemaa et al. 2020, Remote Sensing 12, 3482 and EMD memo on Copernicus DEM accuracy: no sand dune class reported.
- Elhag et al. 2026, Geomatics 6(2), 38 (SBAS InSAR, eastern Saudi Arabia): mostly landfill subsidence; internal unit inconsistencies (for example minus 150 cm/year versus minus 15 cm/year); not used.
- Al-Masrahy and Mountney 2013 dune height, spacing and wavelength trend: already in research/phase2/phase2_morphology.csv (PM29 to PM31), so not repeated; only new findings from that paper were added.
- Statements inside used sources that the authors attribute to earlier work (for example Liwa megadunes up to 160 m in Farrant 2015 and Garzanti 2017; SRTM accuracy figures quoted by Al-Masrahy and Mountney; Shamal wind speeds quoted from an ARAMCO survey in PP1052) were not recorded as findings.
