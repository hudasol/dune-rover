## Inclusion and exclusion reasons

Included:
- Lab measurements on prepared dry or nearly dry sand or sand based simulants (GRC-1, Silica Sand No. 5, Keisa No. 5, beach sand, Tottori dune sand, unspecified loose sand) with a number or a clear qualitative finding: slope climbing outcome and slip, drawbar pull and travel reduction, sinkage, wheel width and grouser effects, push pull locomotion.
- Bekker, Wong and Janosi type parameters measured or identified for sand (bevameter, wheel based identification), and direct shear properties of natural dune sand with full test conditions.
- Model against measurement comparisons (identified terramechanics model on sand; passive earth pressure model for a locked wheel).
- Two simulation only rows (CRM) where they add the Earth side of a Moon result already in the table, labelled simulation-only.
- FJS-1 and Fillite rows only where the same rig was also run on sand or the row documents entrapment, with an explicit warning.
- One moist sand row (aircraft tyre, 6 to 8 percent moisture) as the only open access evidence found on inflation pressure in sand, flagged as not dry.

Excluded:
- JPL TM 33-477 (driven roller on soil slopes): analytic theory, no measurement.
- NASA best practices report (2025): guidance and sample plots; no new measured values in the parts read.
- NASA terramechanics white paper (2022) and the 2023 MaGIC slides: no extractable measurement on sand.
- NASA patent 20190000748 (wheel and grouser designs): not a measurement.
- Shi et al. Frontiers in Robotics and AI 2025 (soft lugged wheel): DEM with 3 mm upscaled particles, simulation only.
- Trans. JSASS 2021 hinge type centre of gravity rover: rock steps on sand, driving force equation garbled.
- Iizuka and Kubota JAEV 2009 (deflection study) and JAEV 2006 (vacuum lunar simulant, scanned PDF without text layer): values only in figures or not extractable.
- Li and Wang 2014: only the experimental Section 3.1 was used; the slip control results are simulation only.
- Two Japanese Transactions of the JSME papers (2015 shear deformation model; 2017 sand flow beneath a wheel): Japanese body text could not be extracted from the PDFs (missing font mapping), so only the English abstracts were readable and no row was written.
- Sutoh et al. JSME 2012 and Yamamoto et al. JSME 2014: results only in figures; Japanese text layers garbled.
- Czapla et al. Sci Rep 2022 (granite, snow) and Lee et al. Sci Rep 2023 (angled spoke robot on river sand, path planning): screened, no usable dry sand wheel measurement in scope.
- Earlier sources already in research/evidence.csv (Inotsume thesis, Ishigami 2007 and 2009, anchor grousers, Carletti, Skonieczny) were not repeated.
