## SUMMARY
Rashid 1 (about 10 kg, four wheels, built by MBRSC) never operated: ispace's Hakuto-R M1 lander failed on 25/26 April 2023. Rashid 2 is built and was shipped to the US in late 2025 for Firefly's Blue Ghost Mission 2 (lunar far side); launch was "2026" in Nov-Dec 2025 coverage but SpaceQ (2026-10-07) reports Firefly lists no earlier than 2027, so no flight data exist. The only UAE slip/sinkage-type data found are Khalifa University KUCARS/MBRSC single-wheel lunar-simulant results for the Rashid wheel (slip 0-0.75, sinkage about 1-2 cm), plus a thesis and IAC paper on wheel/grouser design. Hope is an orbiter. UAE desert ground vehicles (TII, SteerAI xRift, Space42) were shown in 2023-2026 but the retrieved pages give no slip, sinkage or dune-traversability data. The Emirates Robotics Competition is indoor and unrelated to terrain. No UAE/Gulf peer-reviewed dune rover-mobility study was found.
Cross-reference: thermal/dust effects are covered in sibling repo hudasol/dhill.
