## Inclusion and exclusion

Included: sources that state a scaling law or a dimensionless number for wheels (or, for limits of validity, another rotating locomotor) in granular media, read in full, with equations copied from the text and checked against the page image where the text layer was garbled (Slonaker et al. 2017 pp. 2 to 8; Agarwal et al. 2019 pp. 2 to 5; Omura and Ishigami 2024 pp. 2 to 6; Hu et al. 2025 p. 5; Freitag et al. 1970 pp. 113 and 116).

Rows were written only for quantitative or clearly stated findings that are not already in `research/evidence.csv`. Agarwal et al. 2019 produced no new row (its results are rows TM-001 to TM-006). For Slonaker et al. 2017, Omura and Ishigami 2024, Hu et al. 2025 and Freitag et al. 1970, the new rows are different findings and say so in `notes`.

Excluded from rows (kept in the notes with a caveat): Daca and Skonieczny 2022, McBryan 2022 and Knight 1962, because they were read only through a web text extraction tool (no saved file, no reliable page numbers, rendered equations). Excluded entirely: agricultural tire traction papers found in search 19 (agricultural soils, not sand, and they would only restate the Brixius or Wismer and Luth equations second hand).
