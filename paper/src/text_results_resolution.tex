Fig.~\ref{fig:resolution}a shows how detection falls with grid spacing. Every layer is almost perfect at 2\,m (interpolation {{VAL:sw_2_BICUBIC}}) and still good at 10\,m ({{VAL:sw_10_BICUBIC}}); between 10 and 30\,m the AUC of interpolation falls to {{VAL:sw_30_BICUBIC}} and at 60\,m to {{VAL:sw_60_BICUBIC}}, while slope and the detector fall faster. Fig.~\ref{fig:resolution}b explains the fall by face size. At every spacing, faces longer than about four grid cells are found almost always (boosting at 30\,m: {{VAL:rec30_GBM_4}} of faces 4 to 8 cells long), and faces shorter than half a cell are found far less often ({{VAL:rec30_GBM_0}} at 30\,m, {{VAL:rec60_GBM_0}} at 60\,m). Most faces in these fields are short at 30\,m: {{VAL:short30_share}} of the patches are shorter than one 30\,m cell.
