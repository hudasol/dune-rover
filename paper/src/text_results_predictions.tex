Of the 17 predictions registered for this benchmark, 12 were met and 5 were missed. The misses that matter for the reader are those against our own method and expectations: we expected interpolation to add nothing over slope at 30\,m (it added {{VAL:dauc_I30_BICUBIC_vs_SLOPE}} mean AUC and became the best method), we expected the detector to reach an AUC of 0.93 at every site at 10\,m (it reached {{VAL:auc_I10_DET_LittleSahara}} at Little Sahara), and we expected the detector gate to cut the unsafe share at 10\,m by at least 15 points (it cut it by 13). The register, with every outcome and the notes written after each run, is part of the released repository.
