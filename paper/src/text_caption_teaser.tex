What a 30\,m map hides, at Great Sand Dunes. (a) One metre lidar hillshade with face patches (rose) and the 30\,m routes of one registered pair: the slip model alone (dashed) crosses faces that a face map prior (solid) avoids. (b) The 30\,m block mean of the same ground, the map the planner sees. (c) The calibrated interpolation layer computed from it. (d) Lidar profile along the slip model route where it meets a face: seen from an eye 1\,m above the ground 40\,m before the face, the face lies in ground hidden behind the convex brink.
