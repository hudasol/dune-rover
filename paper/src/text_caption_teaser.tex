What a 30\,m map hides, at Great Sand Dunes. (a) One metre lidar hillshade with face patches (rose) and the 30\,m routes of one E1 pair: the slip model alone (dashed) crosses faces that a face map prior (solid) avoids. (b) The 30\,m block mean of the same ground, the map the planner sees. (c) The calibrated interpolation layer computed from it. North is up. (d) Lidar profile along the slip model route where it descends across faces (vertical scale exaggerated): from an eye 1\,m above the ground on the slope above, the face patches lie in ground hidden behind the convex brink.
