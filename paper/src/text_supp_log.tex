Every experiment of this benchmark was designed and given numeric predictions before it ran, and the predictions were committed to the repository (\url{docs/PREDICTIONS.md}) before any metric of that stage was computed. Stages 25 and 26 were the first version of the benchmark; stages 27 and 28 were registered after a blind review of that version and before any of their metrics were computed, when the results of stages 25 and 26 were known. Table~\ref{tab:log} copies every prediction verbatim with its recorded outcome: {{VAL:log_hit}} hits and {{VAL:log_missed}} misses out of {{VAL:log_n}} predictions. Three amendments were made before the affected results were known, each recorded in the log with its reason: the closed loop uses 20 pairs per site, a 300\,m crop margin and the radius and vehicle sensitivities on I30 only (compute budget); after a first tuning attempt was stopped because it would not finish, the nested tuning of boosting fits its inner folds on at most 150\,000 cells; and after a first closed loop run was stopped, with its outcomes unseen, each pair has a budget of 300 replans. Results decided after a run (for example the post hoc analyses of stages 25 and 26, and stages 29 and 30, the post hoc checks of the paper) are labelled as such in the log; the predictions of stages 29 and 30 were written before their runs but carry no confirmatory weight and are not counted above. Correction notes appended after a number audit (rounding of two outcomes and the scope of two others; no scoring changed) are in the log. The log lives in our own repository, not in a public registry, and the six windows had been used in earlier tests of the detector.
