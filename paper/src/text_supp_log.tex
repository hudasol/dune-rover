The benchmark was run in stages, each registered in the repository's prediction log (\url{docs/PREDICTIONS.md}); a stage is one round of design, predictions, runs and recorded outcomes, and its design is split into lettered parts. Stages 25 and 26 were the first version of the benchmark; stages 27 and 28 were registered after a blind review of that version and before any of their metrics were computed, when the results of stages 25 and 26 were known. Stages 29 to 31 are post hoc: they were decided after a review of the paper, and their predictions, though written before the runs, carry no confirmatory weight and are not counted below. Table~\ref{tab:log} copies every prediction verbatim with its recorded outcome: {{VAL:log_hit}} hits and {{VAL:log_missed}} misses out of {{VAL:log_n}} predictions of stages 25 to 28. Three amendments were made before the affected results were known, each recorded in the log with its reason: the closed loop uses 20 pairs per site, a 300\,m crop margin (the local grid covers the bounding box of the pair plus this margin) and the radius and vehicle sensitivities on I30 only (compute budget); after a first tuning attempt was stopped because it would not finish, the nested tuning of boosting fits its inner folds on at most 150\,000 cells; and after a first closed loop run was stopped, with its outcomes unseen, each pair has a budget of 300 replans. Correction notes appended after number audits (rounding of outcomes and the scope of some statements; no scoring changed) are in the log.

Four notes on the outcomes as recorded. The McNemar $p$ values of stage 26 (P26-1, P26-2) treat the 600 pairs as independent; pairs share ground, so these values are not valid inference and the paper does not use them. The baselines of stage 26 (6.2\,\% on I30, 14.7\,\% on I10) differ from those of stage 28 ({{VAL:vc_E1_I30_none_m}}, {{VAL:vc_E1_I10_none_m}}) because stage 26 used the sensitivity vehicle, while stage 28 uses the posterior vehicle. The unsafe share of 92.5\,\% for the detector with the gate in stage 26 and for the slip model alone in stage 28 is a coincidence of two different quantities. The difference 0.045 in D27-10 is computed before rounding (0.8544 against 0.8096). The hit of Y30-2 rests on one pair (35 against 36 of 120) and means nothing on its own.
