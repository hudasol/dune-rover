Table~\ref{tab:detection} gives the mean AUC of every layer for the face map label. On 10\,m block means most layers rank face cells well (interpolation {{VAL:auc_I10_BICUBIC}}, boosting {{VAL:auc_I10_GBM}}, slope {{VAL:auc_I10_SLOPE}}), and R10 behaves like the 10\,m block mean (interpolation {{VAL:auc_R10_BICUBIC}}). At 30\,m every elevation layer loses skill: with default settings interpolation is best ({{VAL:auc_I30_BICUBIC}}), ahead of boosting ({{VAL:auc_I30_GBM}}) and the network ({{VAL:auc_I30_CNN}}), and the detector ({{VAL:auc_I30_DET}}) is above only slope, NAIP features and the aspect rule. Super resolution ({{VAL:auc_I30_SR}}), the network trained four times longer ({{VAL:auc_I30_CNNlong}}) and boosting with five more features ({{VAL:auc_I30_GBMplus}}) do better; nested tuning changes nothing ({{VAL:auc_I30_GBMtuned}}). The 10\,m data still hold the information: the largest of the nine 10\,m interpolation scores in a 30\,m cell reaches {{VAL:auc_I10to30_BICUBIC}}.

Imagery adds a little. Boosting on terrain plus Sentinel-2 features reaches {{VAL:auc_I30_DEMplusS2}} at 30\,m ({{VAL:gain_s2_I30}} over terrain alone), and with NAIP {{VAL:auc_I30_DEMplusNAIP}}; imagery alone is weaker ({{VAL:auc_I30_IMGS2}} and {{VAL:auc_I30_IMGNAIP}}), and at 10\,m it adds nothing ({{VAL:auc_I10_DEMplusS2}}). Super resolution and boosting with Sentinel-2 score above interpolation at all six sites and the detector below it at all six (Table~S3 of the supplement), which with six sites is the strongest evidence such a design can give.

The real 30\,m products are worse than the 30\,m block mean: on their own grids the best layer reaches {{VAL:auc_G30n_GBM}} on GLO-30 and {{VAL:auc_N30_GBM}} on NASADEM, which predates the lidar by about two decades; interpolating GLO-30 to the lidar grid changes little (best {{VAL:auc_G30_best}}). Shifting GLO-30 by the offset that best aligns it with the lidar raises interpolation only from {{VAL:gs_G30_BICUBIC}} to {{VAL:gs_G30shifted_BICUBIC}} (post hoc), so misregistration does not explain the loss; dune migration, the radar's weakness over sand~\cite{martone2016enhancing} and the product's own smoothing remain, and we cannot separate them.

The label matters for small faces. With the earlier cell label (at least 10 steep posts) the 30\,m interpolation scores {{VAL:lab_LCELLn10_BICUBIC}}; requiring 100 or 300 steep posts raises it to {{VAL:lab_LCELLn100_BICUBIC}} and {{VAL:lab_LCELLn300_BICUBIC}}, so a 30\,m map ranks large faces well and small ones poorly; a 30\,degree threshold changes little ({{VAL:lab_LANY30deg_BICUBIC}}), nor do two rules that join fragmented patches (post hoc: {{VAL:rl_H_BICUBIC}} with hysteresis, {{VAL:rl_M_BICUBIC}} with merging), and against faces of at least 60\,m only interpolation reaches {{VAL:rl_L60_BICUBIC}}.
