Table~\ref{tab:detection} gives the mean AUC of every layer for the face map label. On 10\,m block means most layers rank face cells well (interpolation {{VAL:auc_I10_BICUBIC}}, boosting {{VAL:auc_I10_GBM}}, slope {{VAL:auc_I10_SLOPE}}), and R10 behaves like the 10\,m block mean (interpolation {{VAL:auc_R10_BICUBIC}}). At 30\,m every elevation layer loses skill: with default settings interpolation is best ({{VAL:auc_I30_BICUBIC}}), ahead of boosting ({{VAL:auc_I30_GBM}}) and the network ({{VAL:auc_I30_CNN}}), and the detector ({{VAL:auc_I30_DET}}) is above only slope, NAIP features and the aspect rule. Super resolution ({{VAL:auc_I30_SR}}), the network trained four times longer ({{VAL:auc_I30_CNNlong}}), boosting with five more features ({{VAL:auc_I30_GBMplus}}) and the U-Net ({{VAL:un_auc}}) do a little better. The largest of the nine 10\,m interpolation scores in a 30\,m cell reaches {{VAL:auc_I10to30_BICUBIC}}.

Imagery adds a little. Boosting on terrain plus Sentinel-2 features reaches {{VAL:auc_I30_DEMplusS2}} at 30\,m ({{VAL:gain_s2_I30}} over terrain alone), with NAIP {{VAL:auc_I30_DEMplusNAIP}}; imagery alone is weaker ({{VAL:auc_I30_IMGS2}} and {{VAL:auc_I30_IMGNAIP}}; pixel level networks {{VAL:im_IMGCNNS2}} and {{VAL:im_IMGCNNNAIP}}), and at 10\,m it adds nothing ({{VAL:auc_I10_DEMplusS2}}). Paired intervals put super resolution above interpolation at {{VAL:di_SR_above}} sites and boosting with Sentinel-2 at {{VAL:di_DEMplusS2_above}} (supplement Table~S4), but the margins are small.

The real 30\,m products are worse than the 30\,m block mean: the best layer reaches {{VAL:auc_G30n_GBM}} on GLO-30 and {{VAL:auc_N30_GBM}} on NASADEM on their own grids, and {{VAL:pr_A30_GBM}} on AW3D30 and {{VAL:pr_F30_GBM}} on FABDEM. Interpolation scores {{VAL:pr3_A30_BICUBIC}} on AW3D30, {{VAL:pr3_F30_BICUBIC}} on FABDEM and {{VAL:gs3_G30_BICUBIC}} on GLO-30, and shifting GLO-30 by the offset that best aligns it with the lidar raises the last only to {{VAL:gs3_G30shifted_BICUBIC}}, so misregistration does not explain the loss; dune migration, radar weakness over sand~\cite{martone2016enhancing} and smoothing remain.

The label matters. When a 30\,m cell needs 10, 100 or 300 steep posts to be positive (cells with fewer count as negative), interpolation scores {{VAL:lab_LCELLn10_BICUBIC}}, {{VAL:lab_LCELLn100_BICUBIC}} and {{VAL:lab_LCELLn300_BICUBIC}}, so cells with much steep ground are ranked well; a 30\,degree threshold ({{VAL:lab_LANY30deg_BICUBIC}}) and rules that join fragmented patches ({{VAL:rl_H_BICUBIC}}, {{VAL:rl_M_BICUBIC}}) change little, and against faces of at least 60\,m only interpolation reaches {{VAL:rl_L60_BICUBIC}}.
