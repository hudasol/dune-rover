Table~\ref{tab:detection} and Fig.~\ref{fig:auc} summarise the six sites. On 10\,m block means the hazard is largely visible: every method except plane roughness reaches a mean AUC of at least {{VAL:auc_I10_SLOPE_mean}}, and the detector reaches {{VAL:auc_I10_DET_mean}} (range {{VAL:auc_I10_DET_min}} to {{VAL:auc_I10_DET_max}}) without any lidar label. The cubic interpolation and the two learned models are nevertheless slightly better than the detector at every site (mean AUC {{VAL:auc_I10_BICUBIC_mean}}, {{VAL:auc_I10_GBM_mean}} and {{VAL:auc_I10_CNN_mean}}; paired intervals above zero at {{VAL:nbetter_I10_BICUBIC_vs_DET}}, {{VAL:nbetter_I10_GBM_vs_DET}} and {{VAL:nbetter_I10_CNN_vs_DET}} of six sites).

On 30\,m block means every method loses most of its skill. The best mean AUC, {{VAL:auc_I30_BICUBIC_mean}}, belongs to the steepest slope of a cubic interpolation of the coarse surface, a baseline with no training (post hoc paired intervals: better than gradient boosting at {{VAL:bic_vs_GBM_better}} sites and tied at the others; better than the network at {{VAL:bic_vs_CNN_better}} and worse at {{VAL:bic_vs_CNN_worse}}); the gradient boosted trees and the network, trained on lidar of the other five fields, follow at {{VAL:auc_I30_GBM_mean}} and {{VAL:auc_I30_CNN_mean}}. The detector reaches {{VAL:auc_I30_DET_mean}} ({{VAL:auc_I30_DET_min}} to {{VAL:auc_I30_DET_max}}): above the plain slope ({{VAL:auc_I30_SLOPE_mean}}), but below the interpolation and the learned models at every site (significant at {{VAL:nbetter_I30_BICUBIC_vs_DET}}, {{VAL:nbetter_I30_GBM_vs_DET}} and {{VAL:nbetter_I30_CNN_vs_DET}} of six sites against the interpolation, gradient boosting and the network). The detector also underestimates how common faces are: at its threshold of 0.20 it misses {{VAL:miss_020_I30_DET_mean}} of face cells on average, and a recalibration on the other sites lowers its Brier score from {{VAL:brier_I30_DET_mean}} to {{VAL:brier_I30_DET-cal_mean}} but cannot change its ranking.

On the real Copernicus product every method falls again (mean AUC {{VAL:auc_G30_DET_mean}} for the detector, {{VAL:auc_G30_BICUBIC_mean}} for the interpolation, {{VAL:auc_G30_GBM_mean}} and {{VAL:auc_G30_CNN_mean}} for the learned models). A post hoc search for the horizontal shift that best aligns GLO-30 with the lidar gives {{VAL:shift_Dumont}}\,m at Dumont and {{VAL:shift_Kelso}}\,m at Kelso, where the georeferencing therefore agrees, and {{VAL:shift_Algodones}}\,m at Algodones and {{VAL:shift_WhiteSands}}\,m at White Sands, an actively migrating gypsum dune field~\cite{pedersen2015} whose lidar is years younger than the radar acquisition. There the unsupervised scores are at chance ({{VAL:ws_g30_unsup_min}} to {{VAL:ws_g30_unsup_max}}), and only the learned models retain some skill ({{VAL:auc_G30_GBM_WhiteSands}} and {{VAL:auc_G30_CNN_WhiteSands}}).
